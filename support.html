Get in touch

The quickest way to reach us is by email: contact@pokestockapp.uk

We aim to respond as quickly as we can, usually within a couple of days.

Common questions

  • How do I reset my password?
    On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password", enter your email, and you'll receive a reset code. Enter the code in the app to set a new password.
  • How do I become a vendor?
    Tap to apply as a vendor in the app. Applications are reviewed before approval so collectors know every vendor is verified.
  • How do I delete my account?
    You can request account deletion by emailing contact@pokestockapp.uk, or through the account settings in the app.
  • When is the marketplace launching?
    The full in-app marketplace is coming soon. Follow us on Instagram @pokistock for updates.
