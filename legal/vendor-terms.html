3. Listing standards

3.4 Keeping stock current

You are responsible for keeping listings accurate and removing or updating listings promptly when an item is sold, unavailable or cannot be fulfilled.

  • Honour any free-postage or delivery terms displayed on your listing.
  • Provide accurate delivery information and retain reasonable evidence of dispatch and delivery.
