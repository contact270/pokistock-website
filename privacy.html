Privacy Policy.

LAST UPDATED: 18/04/2026

POKISTOCK LTD ("POKISTOCK", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Who we are

POKISTOCK LTD is a company registered in England and Wales. For any privacy questions, contact us at contact@pokestockapp.uk.

Information we collect

  • Account information: when you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password.
  • Vendor information: if you register as a vendor, we collect additional details such as your business name, contact details, and information needed to verify you.
  • Listings and content: information you add to the app, such as listings, photos, prices and wishlists.
  • Usage data: how you use the app, including analytics about pages viewed and features used, to help us improve.
  • Device information: basic technical information such as device type and app version.
  • Location: with your permission, we use your approximate location to show vendors and card shows near you. You can turn this off in your device settings.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To operate the marketplace and connect buyers with vendors
  • To verify vendors
  • To provide customer support
  • To send you important service messages (such as password resets and vendor approvals)
  • To improve the app and understand how it is used
  • To keep POKISTOCK safe and prevent misuse

Payments

When the marketplace payment features launch, payments will be processed by Stripe. POKISTOCK does not store your full card or bank details. Stripe processes payment information under its own privacy policy.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only where necessary:

  • With service providers who help us run POKISTOCK (such as hosting, email delivery and, for payments, Stripe)
  • With vendors and buyers as needed to complete a transaction
  • Where required by law or to protect the safety of our users

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@pokestockapp.uk.

Account deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at contact@pokestockapp.uk, or through the account deletion option in the app.

Children

POKISTOCK is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be notified through the app or by email.
